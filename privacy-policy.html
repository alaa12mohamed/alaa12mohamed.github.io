Shadowfall: Dungeon Legends — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Shadowfall: Dungeon Legends ("the game") handles. The game is made by AD Digital Games ("we", "us").

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no purchases. You can play it fully offline.
  • Your heroes and progress are saved only on your device.
  • The game may contain ads. When an ad is shown, it is provided by Google AdMob, which may collect information about your device as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your save files (heroes, items, progress and settings) are stored only on your phone. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game may contain ads, for example to revive a hero or to double the gold of a run. Ads are shown only when your device is connected to the internet. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset it or delete it in your Android settings);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language);
  • information about the ads you see and tap.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the game asks for your choice through Google's consent message before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time in the game: open the Bag, then the Guide tab, then Privacy settings.

The game asks Google for family-friendly ads only and blocks sensitive categories such as dating, gambling, politics, religion and adult content.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Internet connection check

To know whether an ad can be offered, the game checks if your device is online by contacting a standard Google connectivity address. No personal information is sent with this check.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The game has no in-app purchases and no chat.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
